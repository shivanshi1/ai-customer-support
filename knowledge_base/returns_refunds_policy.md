# Returns and refunds

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for items
in original condition with included accessories. A support specialist reviews
each request before a return is authorized. Do not promise a refund before the
item is received and inspected. Approved refunds are sent to the original
payment method; banks may take five to ten business days to post them. Final-sale
items and digital downloads are not eligible for return.
